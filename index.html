The Evidence-Based Life

Every rule answers two fundamental questions: What does it cost, and what does it return?

Beneficiaries are tiered by return likelihood: 1. You directly; 2. Spouse and direct family; 3. Friends and colleagues; 4. Strangers (where liability and risk must be weighed alongside aid).

You don't need to do everything: this is a prioritized catalog, not an exhaustive checklist. Select one or two rules that matter to your situation. Dotted cross-references can be clicked to preview the referenced rule immediately.

CRITICAL RESUSCITATION PROTOCOLS CH 13
🚨 ADULT CPR: Heel on center chest. 100–120 bpm (Stayin' Alive). Depth: 2–2.4 in (5–6 cm). Full recoil. 30:2 or continuous hands-only.
💨 CHOKING: 5 firm back blows ➔ 5 upward abdominal thrusts (Heimlich). If collapses ➔ lower to floor & begin CPR.
🩸 ARTERIAL BLEEDING: Direct pressure. Apply Tourniquet 2–3 in ABOVE wound (not on joint). Turn windlass until pulse stops. Record time.
🧠 STROKE (F.A.S.T.): Face droop • Arm weakness • Speech slurred • Time: Call 911/112 instantly.
💉 ANAPHYLAXIS: Inject Epinephrine in outer mid-thigh. Hold 3–5 sec. Call 911/112. Can repeat in 5–15 min.
