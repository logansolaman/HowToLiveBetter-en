Why we do not compute "years lost": Each statistic originates from independent epidemiological trials across distinct cohorts. In real life, risk factors (smoking, lack of exercise, poor sleep) frequently cluster together. Multiplying distinct hazard ratios together creates exaggerated mathematical distortions. Each recommendation is therefore presented with its own isolated effect size.
